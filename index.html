Press and hold on the image to get a magnifier, move to the exact spot, then let go to sample it. Switch to “Select an area” and drag a box to analyse only part of the image, for example to leave out the table. If the photo has white paper in it, set it as the white reference to correct the colour cast and exposure.

Colours are grouped with k-means clustering in CIELAB space. Matches use ΔE 2000. Under 2 is a close match, 2–5 is visible side by side, over 5 means the colour has no close Pantone equivalent.

Pantone values here are sRGB screen approximations from open-source lists (npm packages color2library for PANTONE+ Solid Coated and Uncoated, and nearest-pantone for TCX, both MIT). They are not official Pantone data. Newer colours, such as most of the 2000 series, are missing. Add those under My swatches. Screen colour depends on the photo and the display. Always confirm against a physical Pantone guide before going to print.
